What's in the kit

  • Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Duel and Last Man Standing against configurable bots.
  • Touch movement and aiming, primary/secondary fire, zoom, jump and an in-game HUD layout editor.
  • Editable Unity/C# source, project settings, original branding, content/build tools and English buyer guides.
  • Weapon, character, pickup and settings systems, plus a content import pipeline.

Know before you buy

The project is based on my-xonotic 0.1.0-dev.23. Full Xonotic 0.8.6 maps, models, textures, sounds and music are not bundled in the buyer ZIP. The included setup tool downloads and checks the official archive: about 1.24 GB initially, with about 9 GB needed for content plus Unity import/build space.

Setup requires Unity 2022.3.62f3, Android build tooling, Python 3.10+ and Pillow, and C#/Unity development experience. The target is ARM64 Android 8.0/API 26 or newer with OpenGL ES 3. Internet is needed for initial content setup; bot matches are offline after building. No online or LAN multiplayer is included.

No newly built Veylstrike APK is included or claimed. The marketplace gameplay gallery shows the base project's dev.22 build, not the new branding or dev.23 device validation. A fresh Unity import, Android build, signing and real-device QA remain necessary. Known issues include bots sticking on some maps, dark Mortar-model lighting and remaining visual effects issues.
